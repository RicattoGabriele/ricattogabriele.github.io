### 3. Sviluppo (Deep Dive Tecnico, Portfolio & Challenge Risolta)

As detailed in my technical portfolio ([ricattogabriele.github.io/portfolio.pdf](https://ricattogabriele.github.io/portfolio.pdf)), my practical engineering experience centers on resolving non-linear aerothermal challenges from initial concept to hardware definition:

- **Hydrogen Combustion & Flashback Mitigation (H2-EDGE Team):** Within the Politecnico di Torino student team developing an experimental hydrogen combustor, I took charge of the aerothermo-chemical design of the injection assembly. Hydrogen’s high laminar flame speed makes flashback a constant hazard at part-load conditions. To resolve this, I optimized a Lean Direct Injection (LDI) geometry using reactive CFD in Ansys Fluent. By enforcing a refined near-wall boundary layer resolution ($y^+ \sim 1$) coupled with a $k$-$\omega$ SST turbulence closure, I verified that axial boundary velocities consistently outpaced flame propagation across all operating envelopes while avoiding detrimental recirculation pockets at the nozzle exit plane.
- **Design for Metal Additive Manufacturing (PBF/SLM):** Producing miniature, highly-loaded injection passages in Nickel superalloys (Inconel 718 / 316L) imposed strict build-orientation constraints. I re-engineered horizontal cooling channels into self-supporting teardrop cross-sections, eliminating internal overhang collapse and the need for inaccessible sacrificial supports.
- **Altitude-Compensating Aerodynamics (Thesis on Aerospike Nozzles):** In my Bachelor’s thesis on aerospike nozzles, I analyzed the fluid-mechanical interaction of external expansion waves and shock structures under off-design pressure ratios, consolidating advanced skills in compressible aerodynamics, supersonic boundary layer separation, and thermal-structural limits.
