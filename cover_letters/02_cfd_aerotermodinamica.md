### 3. Sviluppo (Deep Dive Tecnico, Portfolio & Challenge Risolta)

As illustrated in my technical portfolio ([ricattogabriele.github.io/portfolio.pdf](https://ricattogabriele.github.io/portfolio.pdf)), I have tackled challenging thermofluid simulations requiring rigorous mesh topology design and advanced turbulence handling:

- **Reactive Flow & Adverse Pressure Gradients (Ansys Fluent):** In designing a low-emissions Lean Direct Injection hydrogen combustor for the H2-EDGE team, predicting recirculation zones behind swirling geometries was critical to flame stabilization. Recognizing the limitations of standard two-equation models in swirling and separating flows, I deployed the $k$-$\omega$ SST model with fully resolved viscous sublayers ($y^+ \le 1$). This setup successfully captured adverse pressure gradient detachment and heat flux peaks along internal walls, which were otherwise underestimated by standard wall functions.
- **Compressible Gas Dynamics & Shock Capture:** During my aerodynamic investigation of aerospike nozzles, I focused on high-Mach internal-external flow interactions. I resolved complex shock diamonds, oblique shock reflections, and expansion fan structures under over-expanded and under-expanded regimes, verifying physical shock detachment against theoretical gas-dynamic equations and numerical schlierens.
- **Convergence & Numerical Diagnostics:** When simulating multi-species reactive flows with stiff chemical kinetics, steady solvers frequently exhibit oscillating residuals caused by physical vortex shedding. Rather than forcing artificial numerical damping, I systematically diagnosed these instabilities through dedicated surface monitors and transitioned to Unsteady RANS (URANS) time-stepping to preserve transient coherent structures.
