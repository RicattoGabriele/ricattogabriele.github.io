### 3. Sviluppo (Deep Dive Tecnico, Portfolio & Challenge Risolta)

As outlined in my technical portfolio ([ricattogabriele.github.io/portfolio.pdf](https://ricattogabriele.github.io/portfolio.pdf)), my experience bridges 3D CAD design, manufacturability optimization, and experimental test campaigns:

- **Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM in Inconel & 316L):** In developing the H2-EDGE hydrogen combustion injector, complex internal passages for premixing and regenerative cooling could only be realized through Powder Bed Fusion (PBF/SLM). I engineered the internal channels to avoid horizontal overhangs by implementing self-supporting teardrop profiles (ensuring overhang angles remained above 45° relative to the build platform). This bypassed the need for internal support structures that would have caused hydraulic blockages and rough flow surfaces.
- **Fluidic Test Bench Architecture & Instrumentation:** Prior to hot-fire testing, cold-flow characterization is critical to map discharge coefficients and spray cone angles. I participated in defining the instrumentation architecture for our experimental test line, sizing pressure transducers, high-temperature thermocouple probes, and pneumatic control valves to monitor flow rates and pressure drops accurately across the feed assembly.
- **Root-Cause Troubleshooting:** When preliminary hydraulic leak tests revealed unexpected micro-porosities at high pressure, I traced the issue to thermal stress gradients and laser scan vector overlaps near sharp contour transitions, iteratively refining the CAD fillet radii and build orientation parameters to achieve hermetic sealing without weight penalties.
